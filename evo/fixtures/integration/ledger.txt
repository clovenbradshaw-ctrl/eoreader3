Records of the Bridgewater Subscription, 1834

Digitization sponsored by the Hartfield Foundation.

The subscription was opened in the spring of 1834 to rebuild the lower bridge, which had been carried off by the floods of the previous winter. Three hundred and twelve pounds were pledged in the first month, the larger part of it by the millers, whose carts could not otherwise cross to the eastern road.

Mr. Aldous Pyke, clerk to the parish, kept the book. His entries are exact to the shilling and note, beside each sum, the trade of the giver: a cooper, two masons, a widow described only as "of the Green." The largest single gift, fifty pounds, came from a Mrs. Sarah Venn, who is recorded nowhere else in the parish papers and whose name the clerk underlined twice.

Work began in August. The accounts close in November with a surplus of four pounds, which the vestry voted to keep against the next flood. The bridge stood for ninety years.
