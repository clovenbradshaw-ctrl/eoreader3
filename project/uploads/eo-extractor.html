The system has already read it and taken notes — who appears, who speaks, what connects to what — and answers by checking those notes and re-reading passages where the exact words matter. Load multiple sources and they appear as chips below — tag which one(s) a question is about; tag a table and prose together and a third pass compares them and flags disagreements. It checks three things before it speaks — every claim is on the page (grounded — by words, by the graph's asserted relations, or by meaning when it's a faithful paraphrase), every part of your question is answered or flagged absent (covers), and a re-reading of the same page doesn't change the answer (stable); the small chip shows all three. It can give a provisional reading while it keeps going, and you can add to your question mid-read — it folds it in. When the page won't carry an answer it will hold rather than invent, and say so.
