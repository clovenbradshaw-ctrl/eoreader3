## Verdicts (the gates, applied)

- **Mechanism A** (adaptive SEM_FLOOR): lift@1 = 0.8x, violate@3 = 62%, embedder abstains 44% of turns. The top-down guess confirms at or below chance — precision-weighting would weight noise, and A collapses to the current fixed floor with extra steps. **Not built.**
- **Mechanisms B and C** (rank by surprise / heat as prediction error): trajectory beats gravity on 42% of argument spans (needs ≥60%), and within-para vs at-break cosine on argument is 0.576 vs 0.373 — the document's own gravity out-predicts the trajectory, and paragraph breaks dominate the surprise tail exactly as feared: deviation-ranking would flag every normal break. **Not built.**
- **Mechanism E** (pre-warm the next region): gated on the same prediction accuracy as A — the next-region guess is poor; pre-warming warms the wrong region. **Not built.**
- **The relation gate**: inversion flagged = true, faithful paraphrase passed = true, battery 16/19 with zero false flags. **The bar passes — built** behind the `relation_gate` rule (off by default): checkRelations/checkRelationsScope, provenance-at-generation via bindClaimKeys, prototyped here and pinned by tests/relation.test.js, with the flag-on parallel golden in tests/golden-relation-gate.json. Its misses share one shape: the true relation was never deposited (serpentine spans, unresolved pronouns) — extraction recall, not gate logic, is the binding constraint.
- **Mechanism D** (envelope on grounding leak): independent of trajectory by design; measured against the claim's own cited span, never the exemplar library. **Built** as groundingEnvelope behind the same flag — the parallel golden shows it catching exactly the costume cites the fallback binder staples on.
- **Stage 4** (prediction error in the reading): gated on read 1 — deferred with A.
