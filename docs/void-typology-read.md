## The conflation counts (these size the build)

- `{{void:term}}` carried **elsewhere ×2** and **invented ×1** — **3 markers conflated** under one bare marker (the worst conflation: the user naming an absent thing vs. the system inventing one).
- `{{absent:…}}` carried **never-set ×1** and **cleared ×0** (never-set is scanned silence + a receipt; cleared is the new destruction-absence terrain).
- NUL acts in the battery: **2**, of which reported as a void: **0** (ambiguous holds and inference links — the material is present, so they must never be voids).

## Verdict

**The split earns its place.** Two different absences shared the one `{{void:term}}` marker (elsewhere vs invented), and the NUL acts are correctly NOT voids — exactly the pairs an auditor and a reader most need separated. Build the four terrains + the fabrication on the typed markers; keep the two acts as NUL.
