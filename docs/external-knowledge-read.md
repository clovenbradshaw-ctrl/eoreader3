writes to the log). This is **step 1** of the external-knowledge stratum: measure the
fix rate on the residual BEFORE building either source. The gate below decides whether
the stratum is worth building at all.

Corpus: ndp, dispatch, liberty, wealth, federalist (2 journalism, 3 essay — the in-repo predictive fixtures).
Network: replay-only (freeze cache); eligible cells read `pending` until a live run freezes them.

Wikidata hit/miss is what separates a real referent from a heading fragment of the same

## What the categories mean

- **contested-coref stall (47)** — a pronoun or ambiguous name pulling between referents *already on the page*. The reader has the candidates; it cannot pick. No dictionary or Wikidata entry resolves which known referent "It"/"she"/"Mrs. Mill" binds to. This is a **discourse/extraction** gap. Fix rate 0 by construction.
- **admission noise (10)** — heading / table-of-contents fragments and capitalized common nouns admitted as entities (e.g. "Departments Should Not Be", "Common Defense Considered" — the Federalist Contents page). A lookup returns nothing or fabricates a referent for noise. This is an **extraction** gap. Fix rate 0, and dangerous if non-zero.
- **abstract→Kind (8, dictionary)** — a single abstract noun mistyped `thing` (Socialism, Subjection, Taxation). The dictionary's noun sense would lift it to `Kind`. This is the **safe** tier (a claim about language) and the only clearly knowledge-shaped typing win on essays.
- **org/law/referent (wikidata)** — a well-formed proper referent mistyped (an org typed `place`, a place typed `thing`). Wikidata could retype it. 2 were suppressed by the **private-individual gate** (courtesy-title persons the document introduces by name).
- **unexpanded alias** — on this corpus the genuine abbreviations (NDP, DMC) are spelled out on the page; the all-caps residual is Roman numerals and ALL-CAPS heading words, not unresolved aliases.

## Verdict (the gate)

- **Knowledge-shaped ceiling: 19/93 = 20%** (floor to build: 30%). Below the floor **even counted as an upper bound** — the residual is dominated by contested coreference and admission noise, which are extraction problems, not knowledge problems. Per the spec, a blanket external stratum here would polish the wrong layer. **Do not build it as a sweep over the residual.**
- **Stalls are the wrong trigger — the load-bearing finding.** The spec aims the lookup at NUL stalls (47 of 93 residual points, 51%). On this corpus every stall is `pronoun-stall:contested` or a contested name — a coreference contest between referents *already on the page*. External knowledge cannot say which known referent "It" / "she" / "Mrs. Mill" binds to. The stall marks confusion about **binding**, not about **identity**, so a gazetteer or alias table earns nothing there. This is structural, not corpus-specific.
- **Admission noise is the second extraction sink (10).** Table-of-contents and heading fragments admitted as entities ("Departments Should Not Be"). A lookup on noise returns nothing or fabricates — the worst outcome for a tool whose whole discipline is the closed world.
- **The dictionary tier is the lone knowledge-shaped, safe candidate (8 eligible).** Abstract noun mistyped `thing` → the dictionary's noun sense lifts it to `Kind` (Socialism, Subjection, Taxation). It is a claim about language, needs none of the world-claim safeguards, and is the only category that is both genuinely a knowledge gap and low-risk. Even so it is **9% of the residual** here — worth building only if it carries on the real corpus.
- **Wikidata's eligible set (11) is unseparated.** It mixes real referents (House of Representatives, State of New York, America, the two NDP orgs typed `place`) with heading fragments of the same surface shape (Same Subject, Proper Form). A **live hit/miss is the only thing that separates them**, and it is `pending` (network blocked here). 2 were already suppressed by the private-individual gate. Defer Wikidata until the dictionary read on the real corpus justifies the world-claim risk.
- **Two blockers to a *measured* (not bounded) fix rate.** (1) The live dictionary and Wikidata APIs are unreachable in this environment (HTTP 403), so every `fix`/`no-fix` cell is `pending`; the harness freezes results on a `--live` run elsewhere. (2) The in-repo journalism is two crafted fixtures, not the user's real articles (the Tennessee / Toronto Life pieces the spec names). The **structural** findings (stalls = coref, residual = noise-dominated, ceiling 20%) are corpus-robust and need neither; the **magnitude** of the dictionary win is exactly what a live run on the real corpus would settle.
